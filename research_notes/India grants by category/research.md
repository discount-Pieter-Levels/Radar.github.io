# Research Grants & Fellowships for Indian Students / Early-Career / Independent Researchers (status as of 3 Nov 2026)

## For each program: name, org, url, summary, region, tags, status, deadline, amount, eligibility, Indian-recipient evidence

### Takeaway
The strongest India-native entries are the IndiaAI Fellowship (open, closes 30 Nov 2026, covers UG/PG/PhD students), ANRF PM-ECRG (the 2026 window may have opened around 2 Nov; sources disagree), ANRF N-PDF (2026 call closed), India Alliance ECF, and BIRAC BIG (for founders with a bachelor's degree). The global corporate PhD fellowships from Google, Qualcomm and Microsoft have India-specific tracks with named Indian recipients. Most global philanthropic funders (LTFF, SFF, Astera, Speculative Tech, NLnet, Sovereign Tech Fund) couldn't be verified as having Indian grantees, or have barriers for Indian applicants.

### Cited Findings

**India-native**

1. **IndiaAI Fellowship (UG/PG/PhD)**: IndiaAI Mission (MeitY). URL: fellowship.indiaai.gov.in. Tags: AI. Region: India. **Status: OPEN, deadline 30 Nov 2026.** Open to UG, PG, integrated dual-degree and PhD students — [Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/jobs/story/indiaai-fellowship-programme-opens-for-ay-2026-27-check-eligibility-funding-deadline-562251-2026-10-17); [India Observers](https://indiaobservers.com/indiaai-fellowship-2026-27-eligibility-application-deadline/). Students get up to ₹2 lakh, per the headline at [India Observers](https://indiaobservers.com/indiaai-fellowship-2026-27-eligibility-application-deadline/). The PhD stipend is unclear: the official page says ₹38,750/month for up to 4 years ([indiaai.gov.in](https://indiaai.gov.in/article/indiaai-mission-announces-fellowships-for-phd-scholars)), but a 2026-27 report says ~₹80,000/month ([CSR Journal](https://thecsrjournal.in/indiaai-fellowship-2026-27-ai-research-support-hindi)). Students must be enrolled at an institution; independent researchers can't apply.
2. **Prime Minister Early Career Research Grant (PM-ECRG)**: ANRF. URL/source: https://anrfonline.in/ANRF/ecrg_anrf. Tags: Deep science, AI, Bio, Hardware. Region: India. Up to ₹60 lakh over 3 years (sources disagree on whether overheads are included); for PhDs in Science/Engineering; age limit 42, with relaxations — [ANRF](https://anrfonline.in/ANRF/ecrg_anrf); [KL Univ notice](https://www.kluniversity.in/rnews.aspx?id=3437). **Status: possibly open.** The 2026 schedule lists a 2 Nov–2 Dec window ([Careers360](https://news.careers360.com/anrf-research-grants-announces-2026-application-schedule-for-jc-bose-grant-npdf-ramanujan-fellowship-other-schemes/amp)); an aggregator says 31 Dec 2026 ([Proposia](https://proposia.ai/open-calls/prime-minister-early-career-research-grant-pm-ecrg-6f803a)). Requires a host institution, which in practice means a faculty position. Launched as one of ANRF's first initiatives (the successor to SERB SRG) — [DST](https://dst.gov.in/node/7852).
3. **ANRF National Post-Doctoral Fellowship (N-PDF)**: ANRF. Source: anrfonline.in. Tags: all science. ~₹80,000/month + HRA plus a research grant for up to 2 years; needs a PhD/MD/MS and a mentor; Indian citizens. **Status: CLOSED.** The 2026 call ran 15 Jan–17 Feb 2026 (one source says 20 Feb); next call likely Jan 2027 (unconfirmed) — [India S&T portal](https://www.indiascienceandtechnology.gov.in/announcementsopportunity/national-post-doctoral-fellowship-n-pdf-2026); [Biotecnika](https://www.biotecnika.org/2026/02/national-postdoctoral-fellowship-n-pdf-anrf-fellowship-for-researchers-apply-now).
4. **DBT/Wellcome India Alliance Early Career Fellowship (Basic / Clinical & Public Health)**: India Alliance. URL: indiaalliance.org. Tags: Bio, Neurotech (biomedical neuroscience qualifies). 5 years, up to ₹2.25 crore; no age or nationality limits; for recent PhDs or those with short postdoc experience — [India S&T portal](https://www.indiascienceandtechnology.gov.in/nurturing-minds/scholarships/faculty-and-scientists/india-alliance-basic-biomedical-research-fellowships-early-career-fellowships-ecf); [IndiaBioscience](https://indiabioscience.org/orgs/the-wellcome-trust-dbt-india-alliance/jobs/early-career-fellowship---basic). **Status: unknown.** A 2026 round is mentioned, possibly closing 16 Mar (unconfirmed); deadlines are posted on indiaalliance.org.
5. **BIRAC Biotechnology Ignition Grant (BIG)**: BIRAC (DBT). URL: birac.nic.in/big.php. Tags: Bio. Up to ₹50 lakh over 18 months for proof-of-concept; minimum qualification is a bachelor's degree, so independent founders and students qualify; applications go through BIG Partner incubators — [CASRAI guide](https://www.casrai.org/guides/birac-big-biotechnology-ignition-grant); [IndiaBioscience](https://indiabioscience.org/grants/biotechnology-ignition-grant-scheme). Calls open twice a year (~1 Jan and ~1 Jul). The latest verified call is Call 25, which closed 30 Nov 2025. **Status: unknown for 2026; probably cyclic.** Next call expected ~Jan 2027.
6. **DST INSPIRE (SHE scholarship / INSPIRE PhD Fellowship)**: DST. URL: online-inspire.gov.in. Tags: Deep science. **SHE: not renewed / suspended in 2026** — [The Wire](https://thewire.in/education/inspire-she-scholarship-for-the-brightest-science-students-will-not-be-renewed-this-year); [Careers360](https://news.careers360.com/dst-inspire-she-scholarship-scrap-no-list-budget-bsc-bs-ms-only-basic-science-scheme-iiser-pune-iacs-kolkata-iisc-bangalore-kvpy). Status of the PhD fellowship: unknown. Recommend listing it as "paused/unclear".
7. **DBT iBEC (pre-iGEM grant)**: DBT. Tags: Bio. A competitive grant for undergraduate Indian iGEM teams, up to ₹10 lakh per team (2016 figure) — [DBT PDF](https://dbtindia.gov.in/sites/default/files/IBEC.pdf). **Status: unknown for 2026.** The iGEM Foundation has announced an iGEM Indian League — [Sakshi Post](https://www.sakshipost.com/news/enterprise/igem-foundation-set-indian-league-boost-synthetic-biology-150254); [iGEM blog](https://blog.igem.org/blog/2025/11/26/empowering-india-through-synthetic-biology-ruchika-tekethotils-igem-journey).

### Inferences
- These best fit students and independent researchers: IndiaAI Fellowship (students), BIRAC BIG (bachelor's plus an incubator), iBEC/iGEM (UG teams), and the corporate PhD fellowships (Google, Qualcomm, NVIDIA). ANRF and India Alliance schemes all require a host institution.
- For the directory's Neurotech tag, the only India-native options are the India Alliance ECF (biomedical neuroscience) and ANRF schemes. I found no India-dedicated neurotech program.

### Gaps
- I didn't search Astera, Speculative Technologies, Survival and Flourishing Fund (only non-Indian grantees came up), Sovereign Tech Fund, Gitcoin, AWS credits, Neurotech Collider or OpenBCI. Status and Indian recipients are unverified, so don't list these without further checks.
- PM-ECRG: the 2026 window is unconfirmed (2 Nov–2 Dec vs 31 Dec). India Alliance 2026 deadlines are unconfirmed. The BIRAC BIG 2026 calls are unconfirmed.
- The 2026 Google India PhD Fellowship recipient list (due ~31 Oct) wasn't found.
- The IndiaAI PhD stipend figures conflict (₹38,750 vs ₹80,000/month).
- I didn't check other DBT, ICMR or CSIR fellowships (e.g., CSIR-JRF, DBT-JRF), or Tinkerers Lab grants.
