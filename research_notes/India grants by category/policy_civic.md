# India Grants — Policy/Civic category (status checked 3 Nov 2026)

## Program list: name, org, url, summary, region, tags, status, deadline, amount, eligibility, Indian recipients

### Takeaway
India-native funded programs dominate: LAMP (PRS), the state CM fellowships, SBI YFI, Gandhi Fellowship, Teach For India, Takshashila NAS and Carnegie India Young Ambassadors. Most 2026 cycles had **closed** by Nov 2026. Two were open or about to open at that date: **Teach For India** (outer deadline 1 Nov 2026, possibly rolling to Mar 2027) and **LAMP 2027-28** (aggregators expect it to open in Nov 2026; not confirmed). Much of the data comes from aggregators, so the writer should flag the figures as needing a check against the official page.

### Cited Findings

**1. LAMP Fellowship** — PRS Legislative Research — https://prsindia.org/lamp (official; eligibility page: https://prsindia.org/lamp/eligibility-and-selection-process)
- Summary: a ~10–11 month placement as legislative assistant to an MP, running from the Monsoon Session to the end of the Budget Session. Starts mid-June with one month of training. Region: India (New Delhi). Tags: legislature, public policy, governance.
- Stipend: Rs 23,000/month (2026-27 figure, per aggregator citing the PRS FAQ) — [agrijob](https://www.agrijob.in/lamp-fellowship-2026-27-work-mp-stipend-eligibility-apply/); [indiascholarships](https://www.indiascholarships.in/scholarships/lamp-fellowship)
- Eligibility: age ≤25. For 2026-27, applicants had to be born on or after 1 Jun 2000. The 2026-27 deadline was 21 Dec 2025, with an online test on 4 Jan 2026 — [lawof.in](https://www.lawof.in/lamp-fellowship-2026-27-i-mentorship-with-mps-prs-legislative-research-apply-by-dec-21-2025)
- Status: 2027-28 cycle "expected to open November 2026" (aggregator, unconfirmed). Mark as **upcoming/unknown**.

**3. SBI Youth for India Fellowship** — SBI Foundation — https://youthforindia.org
- Summary: 13-month fellowship working with NGOs on rural development. Region: India (rural). Tags: rural development, civic, development sector.
- Pays ₹16,000/month plus a ₹1,10,000 completion benefit. Open to graduates aged 21–32 who are citizens of India, Nepal or Bhutan, or OCI/NRI holders. The 2026-27 deadline was 30 Apr 2026, so it is **closed**; the next cycle is likely to open in early 2027 (inference) — [bankersadda](https://www.bankersadda.com/sbi-youth-for-india-fellowship-program-2026/); [outlookindia](https://outlookindia.com/national/sbi-foundation-invites-applications-for-youth-for-india-fellowship-programme-know-details)

**4. Gandhi Fellowship** — Piramal Foundation — https://gandhifellowship.org/apply-now/

**6. Young India Fellowship** — Ashoka University — https://www.ashoka.edu.in/yif-admissions
- It is not a stipend. It is a fee-charging 1-year liberal-studies diploma. Class of 2027 tuition is INR 7,98,000 plus residence of INR 2,05,000. Every admitted fellow receives the Chancellor's Scholarship or at least a 25% tuition waiver, with need-based aid on top — [Ashoka fees page](https://www.ashoka.edu.in/yif-scholarships-and-fee/)
- Final deadline for that cycle was 31 Mar 2026, so **closed** — [YIF application](https://yif-application.ashoka.edu.in/)
- Recommendation: **exclude** under the "funded" criterion, or list it with a note that it is "scholarship-subsidised, not stipended".
