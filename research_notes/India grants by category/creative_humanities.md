# Creative/Humanities grants & fellowships open to Indians (status as of 3 Nov 2026)

## Program list (name, org, url, summary, region, tags, status, deadline, amount, eligibility, Indian-recipient evidence)

### Takeaway
About 12 programs can be listed. Only one cycle is confirmed open right now: the NIF Book Fellowship, which closes in Nov/Dec 2026. Most other India-native programs run one annual cycle a year, and their 2026 rounds have already closed. List those as "closed – annual" and give the usual month they open. All dates below come from 11 searches. I read search snippets and did not fetch the full pages, so check each date on the official page before publishing.

### Cited Findings
**1. IFA – Arts Practice / Arts Research / Arts Platforms grants** (India Foundation for the Arts; https://indiaifa.org). Region: India. Tags: arts, research, performance, visual arts, platforms. Status: closed (annual, several calls through the year). 2026 calls: Explorations closed 30 Apr 2026, up to Rs 1,00,000 for projects up to 6 months; Productions closed 5 Jun 2026 (Tata Trusts–supported); Arts Platforms closed 31 Aug 2026; Folk Culture Museum collaboration closed 4 Sep 2026, up to Rs 3 lakh. Arts Practice grants go up to Rs 5 lakh (aggregator figure). Eligibility: Indian citizens, or people resident in India for 5+ years. — [IFA Explorations](https://indiaifa.org/node/2961); [IFA Productions](https://indiaifa.org/node/3011); [IFA Arts Platforms](https://indiaifa.org/node/3030); [Artists at Risk Connection](https://artistsatriskconnection.org/organization/india-foundation-for-the-arts); [ScholarshipBob](https://www.scholarshipbob.com/scholarship/india-foundation-of-the-arts-ifa-arts-practice-programme-2026-with-up-to-rs-5-00-000-in-funding). **2. NIF Book Fellowship** (New India Foundation; https://newindiafoundation.org/fellowships). Region: India. Tags: writing, nonfiction, history, social science. Status: **OPEN**. Round 13 opened 15 Aug 2026 and now runs every year. Sources give different deadlines: 15 Nov 2026 (Deccan Chronicle), "November 2026" (NIF homepage), "December 2026" (NIF fellowships page), 30 Nov (FundsforNGOs). Amount: Rs 18 lakh for a 1-year fellowship to write an original book. Indian citizens only; OCI holders are not eligible. The separate NIF Translation Fellowship's last call closed 31 Dec 2025. — [NIF](https://www.newindiafoundation.org/); [Deccan Chronicle](https://www.deccanchronicle.com/lifestyle/new-india-foundation-to-open-applications-for-the-thirteenth-round-of-its-coveted-book-fellowship-1978894); [FundsforNGOs](https://www2.fundsforngos.org/?p=487780)

### Inferences
- Strong directory entries: IFA, NIF, MoC fellowships, Serendipity, Inlaks, CWIT, Khoj. Mark them "annual" and give the usual opening month. Approximate pattern: IFA calls run Apr–Sep; MoC June–July; NIF opens 15 Aug; Inlaks in summer.
- RNP and Tata Trusts make grants by invitation or to selected organisations. Exclude them, or list them as "no open call". Tata Trusts' arts money reaches individuals partly through IFA's Productions grant.
- The SNA Yuva Puraskar is an award, not a grant. Include it only if the directory accepts prizes.
