# Open / any-field grants & talent fellowships open to Indians

## Which programs fit the category, with name, org, URL, summary, region, tags, status, deadline, amount, eligibility and Indian-recipient evidence?

### Takeaway
Few India-native programs are truly "fund the person, any field". The strongest fits are Rise (global, ages 15–17, many Indian winners), Thiel Fellowship (global, Indian fellows exist, but in practice founder/builder oriented), Inlaks Shivdasani Fellowship for Social Engagement (India-native, self-designed project), and Ashoka Young Changemakers India (rolling nominations, under 20). Merit/need scholarships (Inlaks scholarship, JN Tata, KC Mahindra, Reliance Foundation) cover many fields but fund degrees, not people. All dates are as of 3 Nov 2026.

### Cited Findings

**1. Rise (Schmidt Futures + Rhodes Trust)**: global; tags: any field, youth, service
- url/source_url: https://www.risefortheworld.org/
- Benefits: a fully funded residential summit, a need-based 4-year post-secondary scholarship (tuition and living stipend), competitive funding for projects, a laptop or tablet, and network access — [Rise](https://www.risefortheworld.org/)
- Eligibility: ages 15–17 — [Pioneer / Rise 2022 coverage](https://www.dailypioneer.com/2022/state-editions/rudraksha-selected-as-global-winner-for-rise-fellowship.html); the official page states no age.
- Indian recipients: 16 Indian students in the 2022 global-winners cohort — [The Hitavada](https://thehitavada.com/Encyc/2022/9/28/Rudraksha-selected-for-Int-l-RISE-Fellowship-Programme.html)
- Status: UNKNOWN. The official page shows no 2026/27 deadline.
- Don't confuse with the JHU "India RISE Fellowship", which is for women in STEMM — [JHU](https://indiainstitute.jhu.edu/our-initiatives/indiarise/eligibility-and-application)

**2. Thiel Fellowship (Thiel Foundation)**: global; tags: builders, dropouts (effectively entrepreneurship/research projects)
- url: https://thielfellowship.org
- Amount: $250,000 over 2 years, starting with the class of 2026; it was previously $100k — [Pulse 2.0](https://pulse2.com/thiel-foundation-2026-thiel-fellowship-class-awarded-250000-grants-to-young-entrepreneurs/), [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thiel_Fellowship)
- Eligibility: 22 or under. Applications are rolling — [Careers360](https://studyabroad.careers360.com/articles/thiel-fellowship)
- Indian recipients: Ritesh Agarwal (OYO) and Diwank Singh Tomer — [Inc42](https://inc42.com/buzz/thiel-20-under-20-two-indians-make-it-to-elite-group/); in 2014, 3 Indian-American fellows plus 1 student from India — [American Bazaar](https://americanbazaaronline.com/2014/06/27/3-indian-american-1-indian-student-among-20-recipients-thiel-fellowships/)
- Status: rolling. Flag: it is framed as for "young entrepreneurs", so it is not a pure any-field program.

**3. Inlaks Shivdasani Fellowship for Social Engagement (Inlaks Foundation)**: India; tags: any field, social engagement, self-designed project
- source_url: https://www.inlaksfoundation.org/inlaks-shivdasani-fellowship-for-social-engagement/
- Summary: 2-year, fully supported, independent learning projects for young Indians — [fundsforngos](https://www2.fundsforngos.org/health/applications-open-for-the-inlaks-fellowship-for-social-engagement-india/)
- Deadline: the last cycle closed 31 Dec 2025 (same source). A ~Dec 2026 deadline for the next cycle is likely but unverified.

**4. Inlaks Shivdasani Scholarships (study abroad)**: India; tags: humanities, social science, science, arts
- source_url: https://www.inlaksfoundation.org/scholarships/
- Amount: up to USD 120,000 (tuition, living, travel, visa, health) — [Inlaks](https://www.inlaksfoundation.org/scholarships/)
- Eligibility: Indian passport holders resident in India, born on/after 1 Jan 1996, with grade minimums. Excluded fields include CS, engineering, MBA and medicine (same source).
- Status: 2026 cycle closed. Not fully any-field.

**5. Ashoka Young Changemakers India (Ashoka)**: India; tags: any field, social change, youth
- url: https://www.ashoka.org/about-ayc ; nomination form: https://forms.ashoka.org/4726607
- Eligibility: under 20 and resident in India. Free to apply. Nominations are open year-round — [Ashoka](https://www.ashoka.org/about-ayc), [form](https://forms.ashoka.org/4726607)
- Amount: none stated. It is a network and recognition program, not a cash grant.
- Status: rolling (nominations). India-specific cycle dates are unknown.

**6. JN Tata Endowment (Tata Trusts)**: India; tags: any field, postgraduate study abroad, loan scholarship
- url: https://www.jntataendowment.org ; Tata Trusts education grants: https://www.tatatrusts.org/our-work/individual-grants-programme/education-grants
- Amount: interest-free loan up to ₹20 lakh, plus a possible gift grant up to ₹7.5 lakh — [indiascholarships.in](https://www.indiascholarships.in/scholarships/jn-tata-endowment-for-higher-education)
- Status: CONFLICTING. Aggregators list 31 Jan 2027 or mid-to-late March. Tata Trusts pages disagree on open vs closed. Typical window is Jan–Mar, so it is likely to open Jan 2027.

**7. K.C. Mahindra Scholarships for PG Studies Abroad (K.C. Mahindra Education Trust)**: India; tags: any field, PG abroad
- source_url: https://kcmet.org/what-we-do-Scholarship-Grants.aspx
- Status: the official page says the 2026 abroad cycle closed 30 Apr 2026 — [KCMET](https://kcmet.org/what-we-do-Scholarship-Grants.aspx). Collegedunia's claimed 30 Apr 2027 date is unverified — [Collegedunia](https://collegedunia.com/scholarship/250-kc-mahindra-scholarship)
- Amount: not verified from the official page in this pass (gap).

**8. Reliance Foundation UG & PG Scholarships**: India; tags: UG any stream, PG in STEM/AI etc.
- url: https://www.scholarships.reliancefoundation.org ; release: https://www.reliancefoundation.org/media/media-release/Reliance_Foundation_Scholarships_2026-27
- Amount: 5,000 UG awards of up to ₹2 lakh and 100 PG awards of up to ₹6 lakh — [Reliance Foundation](https://www.reliancefoundation.org/media/media-release/Reliance_Foundation_Scholarships_2026-27), [Buddy4Study](https://www.buddy4study.com/article/reliance-foundation-pg-scholarships)
- Eligibility: first-year students. UG requires household income under ₹15 lakh.
- Status: the 2026-27 cycle closed 5 Oct 2026 — [Buddy4Study](https://www.buddy4study.com/article/reliance-foundation-pg-scholarships)

**9. Atlas Fellowship**: DEFUNCT. Exclude.
- It used to have an India tier ($12k vs $50k) — [EA Forum](https://forum.effectivealtruism.org/posts/fMrtoKBFK7p6oRHpu/). A wiki says it ceased operating in Nov 2023 — [Longterm Wiki](https://www.longtermwiki.com/grants/nNi-rwpdwb)

**10. Young India Fellowship (Ashoka University)**: India; tags: any discipline, liberal-arts PG diploma
- Every admitted fellow gets at least a 25% scholarship. The class-of-2027 deadline was 31 Mar 2026 — [Ashoka University](https://www.ashoka.edu.in/ashoka-university-marks-15-years-of-the-young-india-fellowship-with-new-diploma-100-spots-and-scholarships-for-all-admissions-open/). This is a fee-paying program, not a grant, so it is a marginal fit.

### Inferences
- Rise and Thiel are the only verified global "fund the person" programs with clear Indian winners. Both fit the directory, with Thiel carrying a builder caveat.
- For the "Open/any field" category, the Inlaks Social Engagement fellowship is the best India-native fit. The degree scholarships (JN Tata, KC Mahindra, Reliance, Inlaks scholarships) may belong in an "education/scholarships" category instead.
- Likely next openings: Inlaks SE (~Dec 2026), JN Tata (Jan–Mar 2027), KC Mahindra (~Mar–Apr 2027), Reliance (~Sep–Oct 2027).

### Gaps
- Not researched (stopped at the tool budget): Neo Scholars, Interact, The Residency, Spark/Uncommon, Encode/Polaris, Pioneer, Aditya Birla scholarships, Infosys Foundation, Nayi Disha, Tata Trusts small grants, "Bharat Fellowship". Neo Scholars and Interact are believed to be US-only. Pioneer has, to my knowledge, shut down. None of this is verified.
- Rise's 2026/27 status and its official age range weren't confirmed. Its fellows list by country wasn't fetched.
- The Thiel 2026 class list wasn't checked for Indian names.
- The KC Mahindra amount and the JN Tata 2027 dates still need verification on the official sites.
