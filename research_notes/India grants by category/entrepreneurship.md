# Entrepreneurship — India grants directory notes (researched 2026-11-03)

Caveat: 11 searches, no direct fetch of the official pages. Most status claims come from search snippets, often via aggregators (startupgrantsindia, incorpx, internshala, opportunitydesk). Every status below needs a check against the official URL before it is published. Excluded per brief: Emergent Ventures India, Atomic, Feather, Nagarathna, Bellare, FOSS United, Kothari, LocalHost India, AI Grants India, FLOSS/fund, Manifund, ACX, Foresight, Lightcone, BlueDot, Coefficient Giving, 1517-list microgrants.

## Q1: Program list (name, org, url, summary, region, tags, status, deadline, terms, Indian evidence)

### Takeaway
About 17 candidates. The India-native government schemes (SISFS, BIG, NIDHI-PRAYAS/EIR, iDEX) are the strongest non-dilutive entries. Talent investors (EF, Antler, SPC) all take equity. Of the global programs, Thiel, Echoing Green and EF have sourced Indian recipients or an Indian presence. Z Fellows does not.

### Cited Findings

**Government (India, non-dilutive or low-dilution)**
- **Startup India Seed Fund Scheme (SISFS)**, DPIIT. url/source: https://seedfund.startupindia.gov.in (official guidelines PDF: https://www.startupindia.gov.in/content/dam/invest-india/Templates/public/Guidelines%20for%20Startup%20India%20Seed%20Fund%20Scheme.pdf). Rs 945 cr, sector-agnostic. Money flows through selected incubators to DPIIT-recognised startups incorporated no more than 2 years ago. Tags: seed, early-stage, govt. Status conflicts: one source says the cycle window closed 31 May 2026 but the scheme is still operational; another says "Ongoing". DPIIT is running a third-party assessment. — [Sarkariyojana](https://sarkariyojana.com/startup-india-seed-fund-scheme/); [FundsForNGOs](https://www2.fundsforngos.org/events/startup-india-seed-fund-scheme-india/); [Guidelines PDF](https://www.startupindia.gov.in/content/dam/invest-india/Templates/public/Guidelines%20for%20Startup%20India%20Seed%20Fund%20Scheme.pdf); [Parliament reply](https://eparlib.sansad.in/bitstream/123456789/2985330/1/AU2428_3dmdiB.pdf)
- **BIRAC Biotechnology Ignition Grant (BIG)**, BIRAC/DBT. url/source: https://birac.nic.in/big.php. Grant for biotech proof-of-concept. Company must be incorporated within 5 years of the call launch. Tags: biotech, deeptech, PoC. The latest call found is Call 25, closed 30 Nov 2025. The official page lists awardees up to BIG-24. No 2026 call found, so status is unknown or between calls. — [Venture Center](https://www.venturecenter.co.in/events/115/support-us); [BIRAC BIG](https://birac.nic.in/big.php); [BIRAC guidelines](https://www.birac.nic.in/desc_new.php?id=1001)
- **NIDHI-PRAYAS**, DST via TBIs. url: https://nidhi.dst.gov.in. Grant to take an idea to prototype over 12 months. Tags: hardware, prototype. Status: "between calls" according to an undated KIIT-TBI listing. — [startupgrantsindia/KIIT-TBI](https://www.startupgrantsindia.com/providers/kiit-tbi/birac-biotechnology-ignition-grant-big)
- **NIDHI-EIR**, DST via TBIs. url: https://nidhi.dst.gov.in. Monthly fellowship for 12 months for aspiring founders (non-dilutive stipend). Status: "between calls", same undated source. — [same](https://www.startupgrantsindia.com/providers/kiit-tbi/birac-biotechnology-ignition-grant-big)
- **iDEX: DISC / Open Challenge / ADITI**, MoD (DIO). url/source: https://idex.gov.in. Defence-innovation grants. DISC 14 offers up to Rs 25 cr (deadline 4 May 2026). DISC 12 re-opened at up to Rs 1.5 cr (15 Jul 2026). The Open Challenge (deadline 30 Sep, reported Rs 15 cr) lets founders bring their own tech. iDEX PRIME goes up to Rs 10 cr. Tags: defence, deeptech, hardware. Status: challenge-based. All 2026 deadlines found have passed, and the official site was last updated 22 Aug 2026. — [iDEX DISC page](https://idex.gov.in/disc-category/48); [Internshala DISC 14](https://internshala.com/competitions/disc-14-defence-india-startup-challenge/); [Internshala Open Challenge](https://internshala.com/competitions/idex-open-challenge-2026/); [StartupFlora guide](https://startupflora.com/blog/idex-challenge-guide-2026)

**Talent investors / pre-idea (India-based; all take equity)**
- **Entrepreneur First (EF) Bangalore**. url: https://www.joinef.com, apply: https://apply.joinef.com/. Pre-team, pre-idea talent investor that pays a stipend while founders find a co-founder. Region: India. Tags: pre-idea, deeptech, cofounder-matching. Stipend was historically $2,000–2,500/month for about 3 months or 14 weeks (old figures). Fall 2026 Bangalore closed 1 May 2026. Winter 2027 (starts Feb 2027) had an early deadline of 5 Oct 2026, with the last 3 months in SF. Status: rolling cohorts. Equity terms not verified. — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/?p=1732406); [YourStory 2019](https://yourstory.com/2019/05/individual-founder-builds-startup-ef-cohort); [startupgrantsindia Winter 2027](https://www.startupgrantsindia.com/entrepreneurs-first-bangalore-winter-2027)
- **Antler India (Residency / AI Residency / Antler Direct)**. url: https://antler.co/location/india. Day-zero residency. Reported terms are Rs 4 cr for about 11% equity plus a Rs 2L grant and $1M+ AI perks; an older structure was Rs 2 cr for 9%. Antler Direct is always open. The AI Residency 2nd edition is marked closed / between cohorts. The fund is $75M (about Rs 600 cr) and made 30 investments in 2024. — [Antler India page](https://antler.co/location/india); [startupgrantsindia Antler](https://www.startupgrantsindia.com/providers/antler-india); [Antler press release](https://www.antler.co/press-releases/antler-india-approaches-final-close-of-maiden-inr-600-crores-usd-75-million-pre-seed-fund); [Antler 2024 blog](https://antler.co/blog/antler-india-announces-record-year-with-30-investments-in-2024)
- **South Park Commons Founder Fellowship**. url: https://www.southparkcommons.com. $1M investment ($400k upfront, $600k in the follow-on), so equity is taken. Spring and Fall cohorts; Fall 2026 opened in summer (aggregator claim). No sponsored visas. India link: ex-Angel One executive Prateek Mehta joined as partner. No India-specific fellowship track found. — [SPC FAQ](https://www.southparkcommons.com/faq); [Inc42](https://inc42.com/buzz/former-myntra-executive-prateek-mehta-joins-south-park-commons-as-partner/); [runfutureproof](https://www.runfutureproof.com/acceleratorhub/programs/south-park-commons-spc-founder-fellowship)
- **100X.VC**. Reported to be India's first iSAFE-note accelerator, with 130+ startups since 2019 (equity via iSAFE). Status and cheque size for 2026 not found. — [Papermark list](https://www.papermark.com/blog/best-startup-accelerators-india.md)
- **IIMA Ventures (formerly CIIE.CO)** and **NSRCEL (IIM Bangalore)**. University incubators and pre-seed investors; NSRCEL runs women-led and student programs. 2026 terms not found. — [Papermark list](https://www.papermark.com/blog/best-startup-accelerators-india.md)
- **Lightspeed India Ascends**. Accelerator for early-stage deeptech; a 2026 edition was referenced. Terms unknown. No program called "Lightspeed Ignite" was found. — [Venture Intelligence](https://news.ventureintelligence.com/private-equity/lightspeed-launches-%E2%80%98india-ascends%E2%80%99-program-to-back-early-stage-deeptech-startups)

**Competitions / social enterprise (India)**
- **Tata Social Enterprise Challenge (TSEC) 2026, 14th edition**, Tata Sons and IIM Calcutta. url: https://www.tatasechallenge.org. National social-enterprise competition; reported Rs 10 cr total prizes (unverified figure). Deadline conflict: 31 Aug 2026 vs 10 Sep 2026. Either way it is closed for 2026. — [Internshala](https://internshala.com/competitions/tata-social-enterprise-challenge-win-₹1-crore-seed-funding/); [startupgrantsindia](https://www.startupgrantsindia.com/tata-social-enterprise-challenge-iimcalcutta); [IIM Cal](https://www.iimcal.ac.in/tsec-chapter)

Startups are funded with equity through the separate Rainmatter Capital. No open call or portal found. Status: rolling/unknown. — [give.do](https://www.give.do/discover/18MS/rainmatter-foundation/); [altss](https://altss.com/profile/rainmatter); [Zerodha Z-Connect](https://zerodha.com/z-connect/rainmatter/approaching-a-decade)

**Global with Indian recipients**
- **Thiel Fellowship**. url: https://thielfellowship.org. $100k grant raised to $250k from the 2026 class (sources conflict: $100k / $200k / $250k). No equity. For people aged 22 or under with no degree. The 2026 class was announced 21 Apr 2026; applications are rolling. Indian evidence: Ritesh Agarwal (OYO) and others; Inc42 reports two Indians in a 20 Under 20 class. No 2025/26 Indian fellow confirmed. — [BusinessWire 2025](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20250522267532/en/Thiel-Foundation-Announces-2025-Class-of-Thiel-Fellows); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thiel_Fellowship); [Inc42](https://inc42.com/buzz/thiel-20-under-20-two-indians-make-it-to-elite-group/); [YourStory](https://yourstory.com/2013/04/oravel-founder-ritesh-agarwal-selected-for-the-final-round-of-20-under-20-thiel-fellowship/)
- **Echoing Green Fellowship**. url: https://echoinggreen.org. $100k over 18 months plus leadership support; open globally. The 2026 cycle closed 8 Oct 2025, drew 4,100+ applicants from 139 countries with India among the top, and fellows were notified Jun/Jul 2026. The next cycle normally opens around September; 2027-cycle status was not verified. Indian evidence: Raju Kendre (Eklavya India Foundation), 2024 Fellow. — [Opportunity Desk](https://opportunitydesk.org/2025/09/17/echoing-green-fellowship-program-2026/); [Mooknayak](https://en.themooknayak.com/education/raju-kendre-of-eklavya-india-foundation-awarded-prestigious-2024-echoing-green-fellowship)
- **Z Fellows**. url: https://www.zfellows.com. One-week program with $10k; one source says the $10k is an investment at a $1B cap, not a grant (unverified). Open worldwide. **No Indian recipient found, so it fails the brief's inclusion rule unless that is verified.** — [Causo guide](https://hub.causo.ai/how-to-apply-to-z-fellows-2026); [getaiperks](https://www.getaiperks.com/zh/ai/z-fellows)

### Inferences
- Truly non-dilutive entries: SISFS (grant portion), BIG, NIDHI-PRAYAS, NIDHI-EIR, iDEX, TSEC, Rainmatter grants, Thiel, Echoing Green. Equity: EF, Antler, SPC, 100X.VC, IIMA Ventures, Lightspeed.
- Government schemes run through incubators (SISFS, NIDHI). Directory entries should link to the official portal and note that you "apply via a host incubator".
